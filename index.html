🔥 Admin Streak

ℹ️ App Guide & How It Works

This admin site monitors daily consistency. The system checks in automatically at midnight to verify activity.


🌟 Integrated Features:
  • 1. Streak Freeze (🛡️): Automatically shields your progress. If you fail to press the button before midnight, a shield is spent to keep your day count safe instead of wiping it out.
  • 2. Milestone Badges (🏆): Celebrates milestones at Days 1, 3, 7, and 30 with digital trophies and confetti showers.
  • 3. Heatmap Log (📅): Provides a visual grid of the trailing 7 days. Completed check-ins flash green, while missed checkpoints turn red.
  • 4. Custom UI Themes (🎨): Lets you shift smoothly between light layouts and high-contrast dark modes via the upper toggle.
  • 5. App Push Notifications (🔔): Keeps a lookout at 9:00 PM local time. If your checkpoint remains unfinished, it pushes an emergency alarm directly to your screen.
